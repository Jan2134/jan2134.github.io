AuraEat

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

AuraEat is a circadian nutrition app that helps you align your eating window with your biology. We are committed to collecting the minimum data necessary to make the app work and never selling or sharing your personal information with third parties.

What we collect and why

Account identity

When you sign in with Apple, Apple provides us with a unique user identifier and, if you choose, an email address (which may be an Apple relay address). We store this solely to link your data to your account so you can restore it if you switch devices or reinstall the app.

Meal logs

When you manually log a meal, we store the grade you selected (A–F) and the timestamp. We do not store photos, AI analysis, or any description of what you ate.

Drink logs

When you log hydration, we store the drink type and amount in millilitres, and the timestamp.

Sleep, heart rate, and HRV

AuraEat reads Sleep Analysis, Heart Rate, and Heart Rate Variability from Apple Health to calculate your personalised eating window and Aura Score. This data never leaves your device. It is processed locally and is never transmitted to our servers.

Eating-window times (only if you turn on the Lock Screen countdown)

The Lock Screen countdown is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, we store the start and end time of your daily eating window, the eating protocol you chose, your time zone and the date, together with the push tokens Apple gives your device for the countdown. We do not store your sleep, heart-rate or HRV readings, or your Aura Score. See Lock Screen countdown below.

What we do not collect

  • Photos or camera data
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Browsing or search history
  • Any data sold to or shared with third parties for advertising

How your data is stored

Data that lives only on your device — HealthKit signals, app settings, streaks — is kept in AuraEat's private app storage, protected by the standard iOS device encryption, and never leaves your phone.

Meal logs and drink logs — and, if you turn on the Lock Screen countdown, your eating-window times and push tokens — are synced to our cloud database hosted by Supabase in the EU. Access is enforced at the database level so only your account can read or write your own records.

Lock Screen countdown (optional)

What it does. In the last 30 minutes of your eating window, AuraEat can show a countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. To start it while the app is closed, our server needs to know when your window ends.

What we process. Your daily eating-window start and end time, eating protocol, time zone and date, and the Apple push tokens for the countdown. Because the eating window is calculated from health data, we treat these times as health data.

Legal basis. Your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)), given with the separate "Lock Screen countdown" switch. It is off by default and AuraEat works fully without it.

Who receives it. Supabase (database, EU) stores it. Apple Push Notification service delivers the countdown to your device and receives the push token and the times shown in the countdown.

Visibility. The countdown is visible on your Lock Screen without unlocking your phone. You can turn Live Activities off for AuraEat at any time in iOS Settings.

How long we keep it. Eating-window times are deleted automatically after 30 days. When you turn the switch off, or delete your account, your stored eating-window times and push tokens are deleted immediately.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete account. Upon deletion, all records linked to your account are permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

You can withdraw any consent at any time in Profile → Privacy & consent. Withdrawal takes effect immediately and does not affect processing that happened before it. Withdrawing the core consent stops AuraEat from working until you agree again.

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data at any time. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change in a material way, we will update this page and notify you inside the app before the changes take effect.
